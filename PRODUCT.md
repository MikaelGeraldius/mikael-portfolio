## Brand and experience
Dark, typographic, motion-led (redesigned 2026-10-30; the earlier olive-on-bone palette is retired). Type does the work: a large serif name, plain sans copy, and one orange that marks the two focus areas and the action; nothing is painted behind text. Anchor links scroll smoothly (interruptible, distance-scaled) and below-the-fold copy reveals as it enters. A typography-led opening gives way to an original, lightweight interactive 3D exploration of AI and web design. The 3D scene is an illustration of his interests, not evidence of a completed project or achievement.
