# Mikael's portfolio — surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode
One-page personal portfolio, web, Experience mode. Recruiters and hiring managers should understand Mikael's education, interests, studied skills, and contact options in a quick scroll.

## Audience, job, proof, and constraints
Audience: people considering Mikael for internships or entry-level work. Job: identify a role fit and contact him. Proof/content: user-confirmed education and studied skills only; two real projects are shown (the song mood classifier and RegionMusic); there is no CV or social profile. Do not invent work, outcomes, endorsements, or mastery. Contact links are included locally; reconfirm before public launch. Static GitHub Pages; no backend. Responsive, keyboard/touch accessible, reduced-motion, no-JS and no-WebGL alternatives.

## Direction contract (dark redesign, 2026-10-30; replaces the olive-on-bone contract)

**THESIS** — Type on graphite (revision 2 retired the earlier "token tiles" device: no backgrounds behind text). The name is the largest thing on the page, a plain sentence under it says what this is, and the one orange marks the two focus areas, AI and web design, and the action. Real work sits in flat work cards, each opening its own project page; no placeholder cards.

**OWN-WORLD** — Graphite fields (`#0e1115` ground, `#151a21` raised, `#090b0e` deep), ink `#eef1f5`, supporting ink `#a7b1be`, one accent `#ff6b3d` as text colour for the focus areas, as the fill of call-to-action buttons, and on the 3D hubs. Gambarino display / General Sans text. Flat, square, no shadow, line, gradient, glow or text background. The 3D topic map is recoloured to the same fields, hubs in the accent.

**STORY** — A recruiter learns who Mikael is and that he is an Informatics student at UMN seeking internship/work in AI and web design, reads his education timeline and the areas he has studied, can explore the two focus areas, and has email as the close. The 3D map illustrates interests, never a claimed project.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Two-line name at 12vw in plain type over a dark ground; the role sentence directly beneath it at lead size with "AI" and "web design" in the accent colour; one accent button "Email Mikael" at its right (below on tablet and phone). No 3D canvas in the opening.

**FORM** — Taste dealt `sentence-as-layout` (seed 823087185); its photograph-dependent hero and sections were not used because the project has no photographs and the brief forbids filler media. Composition stays the earlier `type-only-statement` (seed 1242245214) set pieces, in plain type (revision 2). Impeccable's `concept-seed` and context launcher were unavailable (not permitted to run), so `seed: none (unavailable)`.

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
